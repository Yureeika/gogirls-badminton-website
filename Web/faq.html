Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who can join GoGirls Badminton?

We welcome girls and women of all ages and skill levels, from beginners to advanced players.

2. Where are the training sessions held?

Our sessions are held at [insert your venue name/city]. Check the Contact page for directions.

3. Do I need my own racket?

Yes, players are encouraged to bring their own rackets. Shuttles will be provided during training.

Membership fees vary depending on the program. Please visit the Membership page for details.

Training sessions are conducted weekly, with schedules updated on the Programs page.

Yes! Our players regularly participate in local and national tournaments.

You can reach us through our Contact page, email, or social media channels.
